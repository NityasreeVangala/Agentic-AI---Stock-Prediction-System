# 🟢 Day 36 — Feature Engineering (VERY IMPORTANT)

### What does “feature” mean?

A **feature** = a piece of information the model uses to think.

Right now, the model only sees:

* Price today

That’s like asking:

> “Predict tomorrow using only today, no memory”

❌ Bad idea.

---

## What is Feature Engineering?

It means:

> Creating **extra columns** that describe behavior, not just price

---

## Simple beginner examples

### 1️⃣ Price Change

Instead of only:

```
Close = 1500
```

We add:

```
Price Change = Today Close − Yesterday Close
```

Why?

* Shows momentum
* Rising vs falling behavior

---

### 2️⃣ Percentage Change

```
% Change = (Today − Yesterday) / Yesterday
```

Why?

* ₹10 move matters differently for ₹100 vs ₹2000 stock

---

### 3️⃣ Intraday Range

```
High − Low
```

Why?

* Measures volatility
* Big range = uncertainty

---

### 4️⃣ Candle Direction

```
Bullish = Close > Open
Bearish = Close < Open
```

Why?

* Tells market sentiment

---

## 🔑 Key idea of Day 36

You are **translating finance intuition into numbers**

---

# 🟢 Day 37 — Lag Features (Memory for the model)

### What is a lag?

A **lag** means:

> “Past value”

---

## Why lag features are CRITICAL

Forecasting models **do NOT remember the past automatically**

You must explicitly say:

* What happened yesterday
* What happened last week
* What happened last month

---

## Simple intuition

Imagine asking a human:

> “Predict tomorrow’s price”

They think:

* What happened yesterday?
* What happened recently?
* Is there a trend?

A model cannot do this unless we **feed past data**.

---

## Common lag features

| Feature | Meaning           |
| ------- | ----------------- |
| Lag 1   | Yesterday’s price |
| Lag 5   | Price 5 days ago  |
| Lag 10  | Price 10 days ago |
| Lag 30  | Price 1 month ago |

---

## Example (conceptual)

| Date  | Close | Lag_1 | Lag_5 |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Today | 1500  | 1490  | 1460  |

Now the model sees:

> “Price is rising over time”

---

## 🔑 Key idea of Day 37

Lag features give the model **memory**

No memory = useless forecasting

---

# 🟢 Day 38 — Rolling Statistics (Context, not noise)

### What is “rolling”?

Rolling = calculated over a **moving window**

Example:

* Last 7 days
* Last 30 days

---

## Why rolling stats matter

Daily prices are **noisy**
Rolling stats **smooth noise**

---

## Important rolling features

### 1️⃣ Rolling Mean (Moving Average)

```
7-day average
14-day average
30-day average
```

Why?

* Shows trend direction
* Reduces random jumps

---

### 2️⃣ Rolling Standard Deviation

```
Volatility
```

Why?

* High volatility = risky predictions
* Low volatility = stable

---

### 3️⃣ Rolling Min / Max

```
Support & resistance behavior
```

---

## Simple intuition

Instead of:

> “Price today is 1500”

Model now sees:

> “Price today is above 30-day average → bullish”

---

## 🔑 Key idea of Day 38

Rolling features give **trend awareness**

# 🟢 Day 40 — Sanity Checks & Understanding Failure

Before modeling, you ask:

### 1️⃣ Are there NaNs?

Lag features create empty rows → must handle them

### 2️⃣ Are features leaking future data?

Never use future prices accidentally

### 3️⃣ Are scales reasonable?

Prices vs percentages vs volatility

---

## What Day 40 teaches you

> Forecasting fails more due to **bad data** than bad models
