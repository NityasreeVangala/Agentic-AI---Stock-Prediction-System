The stock market is a collection of markets where stocks, bonds, and other securities are bought and sold. Companies issue shares to raise capital and investors purchase these shares to gain partial ownership. The performance of stocks is influenced by economic indicators, company earnings, and investor sentiment. Financial markets also include commodities, derivatives, and foreign exchange. A bull market is characterized by rising prices and investor confidence, while a bear market is defined by declining prices and pessimism. Investors use fundamental analysis, technical analysis, and quantitative models to make informed decisions.
Trading occurs on exchanges such as the New York Stock Exchange, NASDAQ, and London Stock Exchange. Stocks are classified into sectors like technology, healthcare, finance, and energy. Market capitalization determines the size of a company, while liquidity indicates how easily shares can be bought or sold. Dividends are profits distributed to shareholders, and earnings reports provide insights into a company's financial health.
